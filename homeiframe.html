This is my Honors English II project about themes in the sci-fi dystopian novel Fahrenheit 451 by Ray Bradbury. My project concerns how the theme censorship (and its sub-themes) is portrayed in Fahrenheit 451, interprets what Bradbury's intent / message behind it was, and links it to real-world events such as the Holocaust.

I coded this by hand with HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. I used Canva to make the buttons. I used Ibis Paint X for the sidebar art and to animate the heading, and used Kleki and Canva to draw a couple of blinkies. Inspired by 90s and 2000s websites, particularly Geocities.
