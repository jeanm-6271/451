Censorship in Ray Bradbury's Fahrenheit 451

This is my Honors English II project about themes in the sci-fi dystopian novel Fahrenheit 451 by Ray Bradbury and how those themes relate to real life. My project concerns how the theme censorship (and its sub-themes) is portrayed in Fahrenheit 451, interprets what Bradbury's intent / message behind it was, and links it to real-world events.

The layout is best on computer and not as good on mobile. I coded this by hand with HTML and CSS. I used Canva for making some of the graphics and uploaded them using Filegarden. Inspired by Geocities websites.
